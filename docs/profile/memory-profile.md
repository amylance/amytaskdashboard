## How she works (the operating manual)
- **Understand before executing** — a precondition, not a warm-up: *"I wanna understand
  everything before I could start executing."* / *"I love dissecting everything before I
  get to function."* [mtg]
- **Delegate the how, keep the whether** — *"you're smarter than me… but itemize it, and
  I decide."* Wants each step itemized and approved. [mtg][obs]
- **Reasons backward from a concrete case** — *"if I go into your inbox, I want to
  understand the issue, then I go backwards."* [mtg]
- **Async, message-first** — *"give me the context and the goal and I'll run with it."*
  Prefers calls/check-ins **scheduled ahead**, not sprung. [form]
- **Proactive, not noisy** — *"I will not be annoying, but I'll be proactive."* Anticipates
  Gavin's needs constantly. [mtg]
- **Visual thinker**; captures curiosities for later; risk-averse about breaking things
  (*"so I don't ruin anything"*). [mtg]
- **Purpose-first** — asks for outcomes (transparency, grounding, not-forgetting,
  not-misleading) and lets the mechanism follow. [obs]
- **Holds an instinct open until its reason surfaces**, and will overturn her own model
  when a better one appears (e.g. scheduled brief → on-demand). [obs]
- **"Does that make sense?"** is a *transmission check* — confirming the reasoning landed,
  not seeking approval. [obs]
- **Always acknowledges who helped** — *"Isaac gave me the structure, I modified it."*
  Never "I did this." [obs]
